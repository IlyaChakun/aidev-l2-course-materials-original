# Data Privacy and GDPR Requests

This document explains what customers can do themselves with their data in Lumen, which requests are handled only by the Privacy team, and what the first line is allowed to say. It covers data export, account deletion and formal privacy requests such as access, erasure, data processing agreements and subprocessor lists.

## Overview: self-service and formal requests

Customers have two different ways to deal with personal and business data held in Lumen.

1. **Self-service actions** that Owners can start in the product: a data export and an account deletion.
2. **Formal privacy and legal requests**, such as requests under data protection laws (access, erasure), a data processing agreement (DPA) or the list of subprocessors. These are always handled by the **Privacy team**.

The difference matters for support. If a customer wants to download their data or close their workspace, the first line can explain the steps in the product. If a customer cites a law, asks for a written confirmation, mentions a deadline or asks for a legal document, the ticket goes to the Privacy team.

### Who can do what

Only the workspace Owner can start a data export or an account deletion. Admins, Members and Viewers do not see the relevant settings. If someone other than the Owner writes to support to ask for either action, the operator explains that the Owner has to perform the action or ask the Owner to contact support from the Owner's own account. Support does not carry out these actions on behalf of other people, because it cannot verify who is asking.

## Requesting a data export

A data export gives the workspace Owner a copy of the data stored in the workspace.

1. Sign in as the workspace Owner.
2. Open **Settings -> Privacy -> Request data export**.
3. Confirm the request.
4. Wait for the confirmation email, which contains the link to the ZIP file.

The export is delivered as a **ZIP file within 30 days**. In practice it is usually much faster, and the request is normally completed long before that limit. The 30 days are the outer limit that the Privacy team works to. Operators should state the limit but should not promise a specific day or hour.

### What the ZIP contains

The archive contains the workspace's orders, reports definitions, team member list and settings in machine-readable files. Files are organised in folders by data type, and a short text file at the top of the archive describes each folder. The exact content of the archive is described in the confirmation email.

### Typical situations

| Situation | What the operator does |
|---|---|
| Customer cannot find the Request data export button | Check that they are the Owner; explain the path in Settings |
| Customer asks when the file will arrive | State that it arrives within 30 days and usually much faster |
| Customer wants to export data before cancelling the plan | Explain the export steps; for cancellation see the refund and cancellation policy |
| Customer asks for a data export for a former employee | Escalate to the Privacy team |

A data export is not the same as a report export. Report exports are covered in the reports and exports article and are limited to the content of single reports.

## Account deletion

An Owner can delete a workspace from the privacy settings. Deleting the account removes the workspace, its orders, reports and team members. The process has two stages.

### Grace period

After the Owner confirms deletion, a **30-day grace period** begins. During that time the account is closed for normal use, but the deletion can be cancelled. To cancel, the Owner signs in and confirms that the workspace should be kept. All data is then restored to the state before the request.

### Permanent deletion and backups

When the grace period ends without cancellation, the deletion becomes **permanent**. Data cannot be recovered after this point, even by Lumen staff. Copies of the data in backups are not deleted on the same day. **Backups are purged within 35 days after permanent deletion.** This is why a customer who asks "is everything gone?" should be told that the deletion is permanent after the grace period and that backup copies are removed within the stated period afterwards.

### Before deleting

Customers should be advised to do the following before they start a deletion:

- Request a data export, so that they keep a copy of orders and reports.
- Check whether a paid plan is still running, because deletion of the account does not by itself explain how billing ends; see the refund and cancellation policy.
- Tell the team members, since they lose access to the workspace.

### What first line must not say

The first line must not confirm that a specific account has been deleted or erased. Only the Privacy team can confirm that a deletion has been completed, because confirmation has legal weight. The operator can describe the process in general terms and point to the settings, but must not write "your data has been deleted."

## Formal privacy and legal requests

Some customers do not want to use the self-service tools. They write a formal letter or email that refers to data protection law, or they ask for documents. The following request types belong to the **Privacy team only**:

| Request type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Access | "Send me all personal data you hold about me" |
| Erasure | "Delete all my data under the law" |
| Data processing agreement (DPA) | "We need a signed DPA" |
| Subprocessor list | "Which third parties process our data?" |
| Other legal or privacy questions | Questions about lawful basis, retention duties, data transfers |

### What the first line does

When a ticket contains one of these request types, the operator escalates it to the Privacy team. The operator does not attempt to answer the substance. The escalation reason is one short factual sentence, for example: "Customer requests erasure of personal data under data protection law."

If the customer mentions a legal deadline, the ticket has priority urgent. The priority levels are defined in the escalation and routing policy.

### What the first line must not do

- Confirm that a deletion or erasure has been carried out.
- Give legal advice or interpret a law.
- State a compliance conclusion, such as "Lumen is fully compliant with the regulation."
- Send or promise a DPA or subprocessor list from memory.
- Name how long a specific category of data is legally retained, unless the information is in this document.

### Wording for the customer

A suitable reply acknowledges the request, explains that formal privacy requests are handled by a dedicated team, and says that the ticket has been passed on:

```text
Hello, thank you for your message. Requests of this kind are handled by our
Privacy team, so I have passed your ticket on to them. You will receive a
written reply from the team. The ticket number is in your confirmation email.
```

## Identity and authority

Privacy requests can be misused to obtain data that does not belong to the requester. For that reason, Lumen Labs treats the question of who is asking as part of the request, not as a formality.

### How the Privacy team handles it

The Privacy team checks that the requester is authorised to act for the workspace or the person concerned. This can include asking the Owner of the workspace to confirm the request from the account email. The first line does not run these checks and does not ask customers for identity documents. Customers are never asked for passwords, full card numbers or copies of identity cards in a ticket.

### Third-party requests

A request that comes from someone other than the person or workspace concerned, for example a lawyer, an employee of a customer or a former team member, is escalated without further discussion. The operator does not forward data, does not confirm whether a workspace exists and does not confirm whether a person is a user. A neutral reply simply says that the request has been passed on to the Privacy team.

## Quick reference

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where is the data export? | Settings -> Privacy -> Request data export |
| Who can request it? | Owner only |
| How is it delivered? | As a ZIP file |
| How long can it take? | Up to 30 days, usually much faster |
| How long is the deletion grace period? | 30 days, deletion can be cancelled |
| When are backups purged? | Within 35 days after permanent deletion |
| Who handles access, erasure, DPA, subprocessor list? | The Privacy team only |
| May first line confirm a deletion? | No |
