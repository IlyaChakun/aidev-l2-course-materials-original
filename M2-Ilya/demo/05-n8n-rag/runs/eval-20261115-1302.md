**q01** I clicked 'forgot password' but the reset email never showed up. What should I check?

> If you clicked 'forgot password' but did not receive the reset email, here are some steps to check:
> 
> 1. **Spam/Junk Folder**: Ensure to check your Spam or Junk folder, as well as any Promotions or Updates tabs your email provider may have.
> 2. **Correct Email Address**: Verify that you entered the correct email address associated with your Lumen account.
> 3. **Email Filters**: If you're using a company email, ask your IT administrator if there are any filters that might delay or block automated messages from Lumen.
> 4. **Wait Time**: The email usually arrives within 5 minutes, so ensure you wait that long before requesting another reset email, as multiple requests can invalidate previous links.
> 
> If the email still doesn't arrive after these checks, you may need to contact support for further assistance, but they cannot set or send passwords. 
> 
> Sources: account-and-login.md

**q02** How long does the invitation link stay valid when I add a new teammate?

If the invited person does not accept the invitation within this period, the link will expire, and you can re-send it if needed. Invitations that remain unaccepted will show a status of "Pending" in the team list until they are accepted or revoked.

**q03** Paying by card fails with error E-4012. What does it mean and what can I do?

> The error E-4012 indicates that the payment method was rejected due to a failure or timeout in the 3-D Secure authentication process. This authentication is often required by banks for online card payments and may involve a notification in the banking app, a text message with a code, or a pop-up window.
> 
> Common causes for this error include:
> - The owner did not approve the request in time.
> - The pop-up window was blocked or closed prematurely.
> - The authentication code was entered incorrectly.
> - The banking app was not installed or notifications were turned off.
> - The bank session expired due to inactivity.
> 
> To resolve this, the owner should retry the payment and approve the request promptly. If issues persist, using a different card may be the quickest solution. 
> 
> Sources: payment-errors-reference.md

**q04** I press Export on my monthly report and nothing gets downloaded. Any idea why?

> If your monthly report isn't downloading after pressing Export, it could be due to a few reasons:
> 
> 1. **Export Too Large**: If the report has more rows than your plan allows, you'll see the error **E-2210 "Export too large"**. To resolve this, you can shorten the date range, add filters, or summarize rows using the **Group by** feature.
> 
> 2. **Browser Issues**: Sometimes, the problem is related to the browser. Try performing a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R), using a different browser, or disabling browser extensions that might interfere with the export process.
> 
> 3. **Export Method**: Exports are generated either in your browser for small reports or queued for larger ones. If it's queued, you'll receive an email with a download link, which is valid for 24 hours.
> 
> Make sure to check the number of rows in your report and compare it with the limits for your plan. 
> 
> Sources: reports-and-exports.md

**q05** Alerts about new orders reach my phone 15 to 20 minutes late. How do I fix that?

> To fix the delay in alerts about new orders on your phone, first ensure that you are using the latest version of the Lumen app. If you are on Android and using versions 4.11.0 to 4.11.2, update to version 4.11.3 or later, as earlier versions had known issues with notification delays.
> 
> Next, check your phone's battery optimization settings. Go to **Settings -> Notifications** in the Lumen app and ensure push notifications are enabled. Then, in your phone's system settings, navigate to **Apps -> Lumen -> Battery** and set it to **Unrestricted** or **Don't optimize**. Additionally, verify that notifications are allowed and not set to silent. Restart your phone and test if the notifications arrive promptly.
> 
> If the problem persists, it may be due to battery management settings that restrict background activity. 
> 
> Sources: release-notes-and-known-issues.md, notifications-and-alerts.md

**q06** How many API calls per minute am I allowed on the Team plan?

**q09** After I delete my workspace, how long is my data still kept anywhere?

> After you delete your workspace, there is a **30-day grace period** during which the deletion can be canceled. If not canceled, the deletion becomes **permanent**, and data cannot be recovered. However, copies of the data in backups are retained and purged within **35 days** after the permanent deletion. Therefore, while the workspace data is permanently deleted after the grace period, backup copies will still exist for a short time afterward.
> 
> Sources: data-privacy-and-gdpr-requests.md, refund-and-cancellation-policy.md

**m02** I want to cancel my annual subscription and take all my data with me. What do I do and in which order?

> To cancel your annual subscription and take your data with you, follow these steps:
> 
> 1. **Export Your Data**: Before cancelling, sign in as the workspace Owner and go to **Settings -> Privacy -> Request data export**. Confirm the request and wait for the confirmation email with a link to download your data as a ZIP file, which will be delivered within 30 days.
> 
> 2. **Cancel Your Subscription**: After exporting your data, still signed in as the Owner, navigate to **Settings -> Billing -> Cancel plan**. Read the summary of when access will end and confirm the cancellation.
> 
> Remember, cancelling stops future charges but does not refund the current paid period; you will retain access until the end of that period.
>
