**q01** I clicked 'forgot password' but the reset email never showed up. What should I check?

> If you clicked 'forgot password' but did not receive the reset email, here are some steps to check:
> 
> 1. Ensure you check your Spam or Junk folder, as well as any Promotions or Updates tabs your email provider may have.
> 2. Verify that you entered the correct email address associated with your account.
> 3. If you are using a company email, consult your IT administrator to see if their system filters or delays automated messages.
> 4. Wait for at least 5 minutes before requesting another reset email, as multiple requests can invalidate earlier links.
> 
> If the email still does not arrive after these checks, you may need to contact support for further assistance, but they cannot set or send passwords. 
> 
> Sources: account-and-login.md

**q02** How long does the invitation link stay valid when I add a new teammate?

> The invitation link for adding a new teammate is valid for **7 days**. If the invited person does not accept the invitation within this period, the link will expire, and you will need to resend it. You can resend the invitation at any time while it is pending, and if more than 7 days have passed, you will need to send a new invitation. 
> 
> Sources: team-and-roles.md, team-and-roles.md

**q03** Paying by card fails with error E-4012. What does it mean and what can I do?

> The error E-4012 indicates that the payment method was rejected due to a failure or timeout in the 3-D Secure authentication process. This authentication is often required by banks for online card payments and may involve a notification in the banking app, a text message with a code, or a pop-up window.
> 
> Common causes for this error include:
> - The owner did not approve the request in time.
> - The pop-up window was blocked or closed prematurely.
> - The authentication code was entered incorrectly.
> - The banking app was not installed or notifications were turned off.
> 
> To resolve this, the owner should retry the payment and approve the request promptly. If issues persist, using a different card may be advisable. 
> 
> Sources: payment-errors-reference.md

**q04** I press Export on my monthly report and nothing gets downloaded. Any idea why?

> If your monthly report export is not downloading, it could be due to a few reasons. One common issue is that the report may exceed the row limit allowed by your plan, resulting in an error labeled **E-2210 "Export too large."** To resolve this, you can try shortening the date range, adding filters, or summarizing rows using the **Group by** feature.
> 
> Additionally, if the export button is clicked but nothing happens, it might be a browser-related issue. You can try refreshing the page, using a different browser, or disabling browser extensions that may interfere with the export process. 
> 
> Sources: reports-and-exports.md

**q05** Alerts about new orders reach my phone 15 to 20 minutes late. How do I fix that?

> To fix the delay in alerts about new orders on your phone, first ensure that you are using the latest version of the Lumen app. If you are on an Android device, update to version 4.11.3 or later, as earlier versions had known issues with notification delays. 
> 
> Additionally, check your phone's battery optimization settings. Go to **Settings -> Notifications** in the Lumen app and ensure push notifications are enabled. Then, in your phone's system settings, navigate to **Apps -> Lumen -> Battery** and set it to **Unrestricted** or **Don't optimize**. Also, verify that notifications are allowed and not set to silent. 
> 
> If the problem persists, consider checking for any Focus modes or Background App Refresh settings that might be affecting notifications. 
> 
> Sources: release-notes-and-known-issues.md, notifications-and-alerts.md

**q06** How many API calls per minute am I allowed on the Team plan?

**q07** I bought a yearly plan two months ago and changed my mind. Can I still get my money back?

Since you purchased your plan two months ago, you are no longer eligible for a refund, as the 30-day money-back window has passed. After this period, there are no refunds or prorated refunds available, and your plan will continue until the end of the term.

**q09** After I delete my workspace, how long is my data still kept anywhere?

> After you delete your workspace, there is a **30-day grace period** during which the deletion can be canceled. If not canceled, the deletion becomes **permanent**, and data cannot be recovered. However, copies of the data in backups are retained and purged within **35 days** after the permanent deletion. Therefore, while the workspace data is permanently deleted after the grace period, backup copies remain for a short time afterward.
> 
> Sources: data-privacy-and-gdpr-requests.md

**m02** I want to cancel my annual subscription and take all my data with me. What do I do and in which order?

> To cancel your annual subscription and take your data with you, follow these steps:
> 
> 1. **Export Your Data**: Before cancelling, sign in as the workspace Owner and go to **Settings -> Privacy -> Request data export**. Confirm the request and wait for the confirmation email with a link to download your data as a ZIP file, which will be delivered within 30 days.
> 
> 2. **Cancel Your Subscription**: After exporting your data, still signed in as the Owner, navigate to **Settings -> Billing -> Cancel plan**. Read the summary of when access will end and confirm the cancellation.
> 
> Remember, cancelling stops future charges but does not refund the current paid period; you will retain access until the end of that period.
> 
> Sources: refund-and-cancellation-policy.md, data-privacy-and-gdpr-requests.md
