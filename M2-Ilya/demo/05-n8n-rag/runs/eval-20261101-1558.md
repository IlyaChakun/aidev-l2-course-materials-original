**q01** I clicked 'forgot password' but the reset email never showed up. What should I check?

> If you clicked 'forgot password' but did not receive the reset email, here are some steps to check:
> 
> 1. Ensure you check your Spam or Junk folder, as well as any Promotions or Updates tabs your email provider may have.
> 2. Verify that you entered the correct email address associated with your Lumen account.
> 3. If you are using a company email, ask your IT administrator if there are any filters or delays for automated messages.
> 4. Wait for at least 5 minutes before requesting another reset email, as multiple requests can invalidate earlier links.
> 
> If the email still does not arrive after these checks, you may need to contact support for further assistance, but they cannot set or send passwords. 
> 
> Sources: account-and-login.md

**q02** How long does the invitation link stay valid when I add a new teammate?

> The invitation link for adding a new teammate is valid for **7 days**. If the invited person does not accept the invitation within this period, the link will expire, and you will need to re-send it. You can re-send the invitation at any time while it is pending, and if more than 7 days have passed, you will need to send a new invitation. 
> 
> Sources: team-and-roles.md, account-and-login.md

**q03** Paying by card fails with error E-4012. What does it mean and what can I do?

> The error E-4012 indicates that the payment method was rejected due to a failure or timeout in the 3-D Secure authentication process. This is an additional security step that many banks require for online card payments. Common causes include not approving the request in time, browser pop-ups being blocked, or incorrect entry of the authentication code.
> 
> To resolve this issue, the card owner should retry the payment and approve the request promptly in their banking app. If the pop-up is blocked, they should allow pop-ups for the Lumen site and try again. If the problem persists, using a different card may be the quickest solution. 
> 
> Sources: payment-errors-reference.md

**q04** I press Export on my monthly report and nothing gets downloaded. Any idea why?

> If your monthly report isn't downloading after pressing Export, it could be due to a few reasons. One common issue is that the report may exceed the row limit allowed by your plan, resulting in an error labeled **E-2210 "Export too large."** To resolve this, you can try shortening the date range, adding filters, or summarizing rows using the **Group by** feature.
> 
> Additionally, if the export button is clicked but nothing happens, it might be a browser-related issue. You can try refreshing the page, using a different browser, or disabling any browser extensions that might interfere with the export process. 
> 
> Make sure to check the number of rows displayed at the bottom of the report and compare it with the limits specified for your plan. 
> 
> Sources: reports-and-exports.md

**q05** Alerts about new orders reach my phone 15 to 20 minutes late. How do I fix that?

> To fix the delay in alerts about new orders on your phone, follow these steps:
> 
> 1. **Update the App**: Ensure your Android app is updated to version 4.11.3 or later, as earlier versions had known issues causing delays.
> 2. **Check Settings**: Go to **Settings -> Notifications** in the Lumen app and ensure push notifications are enabled.
> 3. **Battery Optimization**: In your phone's settings, navigate to **Apps -> Lumen -> Battery** and set it to **Unrestricted** or **Don't optimize**.
> 4. **Notification Permissions**: Ensure notifications are allowed and not set to silent.
> 5. **Additional Settings**: If your phone has an app launch or auto-start manager, allow Lumen there, and check that Do Not Disturb is not active during expected notification times.
> 6. **Restart and Test**: Restart your phone and wait for the next order to see if notifications arrive promptly.
> 
> These steps should help resolve the issue, as delays are often caused by battery management settings. 
> 
> Sources: release-notes-and-known-issues.md, notifications-and-alerts.md

**q06** How many API calls per minute am I allowed on the Team plan?

**q07** I bought a yearly plan two months ago and changed my mind. Can I still get my money back?

Since you purchased your plan two months ago, you are no longer eligible for a refund, as the 30-day money-back window has passed. After this period, there are no refunds or prorated refunds available, and your plan will continue until the end of the term.

**q09** After I delete my workspace, how long is my data still kept anywhere?

> After you delete your workspace, there is a **30-day grace period** during which the deletion can be canceled. If not canceled, the deletion becomes **permanent**, and data cannot be recovered. However, backups of the data are purged within **35 days** after the permanent deletion. Therefore, while the workspace data is permanently deleted after the grace period, backup copies remain for a short time before being removed.
> 
> Sources: data-privacy-and-gdpr-requests.md, refund-and-cancellation-policy.md

**m02** I want to cancel my annual subscription and take all my data with me. What do I do and in which order?

> To cancel your annual subscription and take your data with you, follow these steps:
> 
> 1. **Export Your Data**: Before cancelling, sign in as the workspace Owner and go to **Settings -> Privacy -> Request data export**. Confirm the request and wait for the confirmation email with a link to download your data as a ZIP file, which will be delivered within 30 days.
> 
> 2. **Cancel Your Subscription**: After exporting your data, still signed in as the Owner, navigate to **Settings -> Billing -> Cancel plan**. Read the summary of when access will end and confirm the cancellation.
> 
> Remember, cancelling stops future charges but does not refund the current paid period; you will retain access until the end of that period.
> 
> Sources: refund-and-cancellation-policy.md, data-privacy-and-gdpr-requests.md
