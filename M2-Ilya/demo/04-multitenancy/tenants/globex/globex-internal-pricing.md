# Globex Internal Pricing Exceptions

This page is restricted to the Globex Inc finance group. It describes the confidential pricing levers for Globex Warehouse contracts. Nothing on this page may be shared with customers or with the support team.

## VIP discount code

The VIP discount code **GLOBEX-VIP-2026** gives **50% off the first six months** of any Business plan. Finance applies it manually for strategic accounts that are migrating from a competitor. The code is single use and is valid until 30 November 2026. When a customer asks support for a discount code, support must answer that discounts exist only through a signed order form and must not mention this code.

## Floor price and approvals

The floor price for Globex Warehouse Business is **$74 per month**. Deals below the floor require approval from the VP of Finance and a note in the contract record. Multi-year deals may combine the VIP code with annual billing.

## Margin notes

Internal target gross margin for Globex Warehouse is 77%. The VIP code is budgeted as a customer acquisition cost, not as a price change.
