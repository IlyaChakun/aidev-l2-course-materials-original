# Acme Orders Fulfilment Pilot

This article describes the fulfilment pilot that Acme Corp runs with a small group of customers. It explains who can join, what changes in the app, and where to send feedback.

## Pilot overview

In the pilot, orders that a customer marks as ready are sent directly to the partner warehouse. The warehouse confirms each shipment, and the order status changes to Shipped without manual input. Customers in the pilot see a new Fulfilment column on the Orders page. The pilot runs until the end of the year and is free of charge for the participating workspaces.

## Who can join

Any workspace on the Team or Business plan can ask to join. The Owner sends a request through the Help menu, and the product team confirms within five business days. Workspaces on Starter cannot join, because the pilot uses the API.

## Feedback

Feedback goes to the product team through the Help menu with the label "fulfilment pilot". Support agents collect the workspace name, the order number and a screenshot, and then pass the feedback on without promising changes.
